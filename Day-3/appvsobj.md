What is Salesforce CRM?

Salesforce is a cloud-based Customer Relationship Management (CRM) platform that helps businesses manage customer interactions, sales activities, and business operations efficiently.

 Managing Prospects in Salesforce

 Understanding Prospects

A prospect is a person or organization that may become a future customer.

Adding Prospect Details

Users can store information such as:

* Full Name
* Organization Name
* Mobile Number
* Email Address
* Prospect Stage

 Prospect Stages

Common stages include:

* New
* Contacted
* Interested
* Closed

---

Prospect Conversion Process

 What Happens After Conversion?

When a prospect shows strong interest, Salesforce converts it into:

 Client Account

Represents the company or business.

 Customer Contact

Represents the individual associated with the company.

 Sales Deal

Represents the ongoing business opportunity or transaction.

 Salesforce Process Flow

**Prospect → Account + Contact + Deal**

This flow represents how Salesforce converts potential customers into business opportunities and customer relationships.
