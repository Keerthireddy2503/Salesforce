What is CRM?
CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a system that helps businesses manage customer information, sales, marketing, and customer support in one place. It helps companies build better relationships with customers and improve business performance.
Salesforce Customer 360 is a CRM platform that connects all customer data, teams, AI, and automation on a single platform.

CRM helps businesses:
Understand customer needs
Improve communication and customer relationships
Increase sales and productivity
Manage customer support efficiently
