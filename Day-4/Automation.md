Why Automation Matters

Automation helps businesses perform tasks automatically without manual work. It saves time, reduces errors, and increases productivity.

Importance of Automation

Reduces repetitive work

Saves time and effort

Improves accuracy

Increases efficiency

Speeds up business processes


Example

Instead of manually sending fee reminders to students, Salesforce can automatically send emails using automation.


---

Different Automation Types

Salesforce provides different automation tools for different tasks.

1. Flow Builder

Used to automate processes using clicks instead of code.

Example:
Automatically sending attendance warning emails.


---

2. Validation Rules

Used to check whether entered data is correct.

Example:
Preventing students from entering invalid phone numbers.


---

3. Approval Process

Used when records need approval from higher authorities.

Example:
Leave requests needing manager approval.


---

4. Email Alerts

Automatically sends emails based on conditions.

Example:
Sending order confirmation emails to customers.


---

5. Scheduled Automation

Runs automation at a fixed time.

Example:
Generating monthly reports automatically.


---

Benefits

Faster task completion
